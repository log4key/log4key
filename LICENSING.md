# Licensing Overview for Log4Key

Log4Key follows a **dual-licensing model** designed to balance open collaboration with sustainable commercial development.

Log4Key is available through the following licensing options:

- 🧑‍💻 **Community users**: Free use under **GNU GPLv3**  
- 🚀 **Startups**: Free **Startup License** (no GPL restrictions)  
- 🏢 **Enterprises**: Custom **Commercial License** (paid)

This approach keeps the core logging infrastructure open while supporting the long-term sustainability of the project.

---

## 1. Community Edition (GPLv3)

The source code in this repository is licensed under the **GNU General Public License v3.0** (see [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt)).

### Who it’s for:
- Individual developers  
- Open source projects  
- Companies comfortable with GPLv3 obligations

### Key terms:
- You may use, modify, and distribute Log4Key freely.
- If you distribute Log4Key, or a modified version of Log4Key, you must comply with the terms of GPLv3.
- This typically means providing source code for Log4Key and any modifications you make when redistributing it.

> ℹ️ The community edition includes all core features: key-based log routing, multi-target output (file, console, network), and lightweight embedding.

## 2. Startup License (Free)

To support early-stage innovation, we offer a **free Startup License** to qualifying startups.

### Who qualifies:
- Company less than **3 years old**, **and**  
- Total funding raised **under $2 million USD** **or** annual revenue **under $2 million USD**, **and**  
- Fewer than **50 employees**

### Benefits:
- Use Log4Key in **proprietary, closed-source products**  
- No requirement to disclose your source code  
- Same core functionality as the community edition  
- Full rights granted under the [STARTUP_LICENSE.md](STARTUP_LICENSE.md)

### How to get it:
1. Submit a request to [legal@log4key.com](mailto:legal@log4key.com)  
2. Provide reasonable proof of eligibility (e.g., incorporation date, funding information, or team size)  
3. Receive your Startup License grant within 3 business days

> ⚠️ If the eligibility criteria are no longer met, organizations must transition to a commercial license for continued use in proprietary products.

## 4. How Contributions Enable Dual Licensing

All external contributions are accepted under a **Contributor License Agreement (CLA)**:

- [Individual CLA (ICLA.md)](ICLA.md)  
- [Corporate CLA (CCLA.md)](CCLA.md)

By signing the CLA, contributors:
- Retain copyright to their code  
- Grant Log4Key the right to distribute their contribution under **both GPLv3 and commercial licenses**

This ensures every line of code in the project can legally appear in **both the community and commercial editions**.

> 💡 Learn more about contributing: [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)

## FAQ

### Can I use Log4Key in my SaaS product?

Yes.  
You may embed Log4Key within your own applications, including SaaS platforms, observability pipelines, or internal infrastructure.

However, offering Log4Key as a standalone **Log Routing-as-a-Service (LRaaS)** product requires a commercial license.

### Do I need to sign a CLA to use Log4Key?

No.  
The CLA is only required if you **contribute code** to the project.  
Using Log4Key (under GPLv3, Startup License, or Commercial License) does **not** require signing a CLA.

### Is the community edition feature-limited?

No.  
The community edition includes all core functionality. Future advanced features may be offered under commercial editions while keeping the core logging infrastructure open.
